04 Technical FAQ

Clean-in-Place Systems — Questions & Answers

Answers to common project scoping, chemical recovery, and skid automation questions.

What tank configurations are available for APS CIP skids?

APS engineers 2-tank (rinse and caustic), 3-tank (water, caustic, acid), 4-tank (fresh water, caustic, acid, recovered rinse), and 5-tank systems with dedicated hot water and final sanitizer vessels, sized from 500 liters up to 10,000 liters per tank.

How does automated chemical recovery reduce operating costs?

Using inline toroidal conductivity sensors, return CIP fluids are automatically analyzed. Once detergent concentrations match target thresholds, pneumatic valves divert the solution back into the recovery tank rather than discharging to drain, saving up to 70% of caustic detergent and freshwater.

What heating options are integrated into CIP skids?

Skids can be equipped with plate heat exchangers or shell-and-tube heat exchangers powered by plant steam, direct steam injection nozzles, or integrated electric immersion heaters for facilities without a steam boiler.
