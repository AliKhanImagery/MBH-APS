01 Process Modules

Engineered Modules for Industrial Water Treatment

Modular purification stages configured to protect sensitive process machinery, boiler feeds, and beverage recipes.

Stage 01

Industrial Reverse Osmosis

High-rejection spiral-wound membrane skids removing dissolved solids, heavy metals, and silica for food, beverage, and manufacturing utilities.

  • 8-inch high-rejection polyamide TFC membranes
  • Duplex stainless steel 2205 / AISI 316L high-pressure piping
  • VFD-driven multi-stage centrifugal booster pumps (Grundfos/Danfoss)
  • Automated low-pressure permeate flush & CIP recirculation
Capacities: 2,000 to 100,000 LPH permeate output
Stage 02

Multimedia & Carbon Pre-Filtration

Automated pre-treatment vessels removing suspended solids, chlorine, organics, and turbidity to safeguard downstream RO membranes.

  • FRP or epoxy-coated / AISI 316L stainless pressure vessels
  • Multi-layer graded quartz sand, anthracite, & garnet media
  • Granular activated carbon (GAC) for chlorine & odor eradication
  • Pneumatically actuated backwash manifold with differential pressure triggers
Turbidity Reduction: < 0.1 NTU / SDI < 3.0
Stage 03

UV Disinfection & Dosing

Chemical-free microbial inactivation and precise chemical dosing skids ensuring biological safety without altering product taste.

  • Electro-polished AISI 316L inline UV disinfection chambers (Ra < 0.4 µm)
  • High-output 254 nm germicidal amalgam UV lamps with UV intensity sensors
  • Proportional chemical dosing (anti-scalant, SMBS, pH correction)
  • Automated wiper mechanism preventing quartz sleeve fouling
Pathogen Inactivation: 99.99% (4-log reduction)

04 Technical FAQ

Industrial Water Treatment — Questions & Answers

Answers to common project scoping, membrane selection, and water recovery questions.

What purification stages are included in an APS industrial water treatment system?

Complete APS water treatment systems integrate raw water pre-filtration (sand, activated carbon, manganese greensand), chemical conditioning (anti-scalant, sodium bisulfite dosing), 5-micron cartridge filtration, high-pressure spiral-wound reverse osmosis (RO) membranes, and final UV disinfection with permeate storage.

What membrane recovery and salt rejection rates do APS RO skids achieve?

Our industrial brackish water reverse osmosis (BWRO) and seawater reverse osmosis (SWRO) skids achieve 99.2% to 99.7% salt rejection with recovery rates between 75% and 85% on brackish feed, configured with multi-stage array passes to optimize energy efficiency.

How are RO membranes protected from organic and mineral fouling?

Membranes are protected by precision anti-scalant proportional dosing, automatic low-pressure permeate flush cycles upon line stop, and dedicated skid-mounted clean-in-place (CIP) membrane cleaning tanks with chemical recirculation heating.

What is the technical quotation timeline for an industrial RO skid tender?

Our engineering desk reviews your raw water lab analysis (TDS, silica, hardness, conductivity), required permeate output (m3/day or LPH), and intended application to deliver an engineered technical proposal and budget within 48 hours.
