01 Process Modules

Engineered Modules for Beverage Syrup Preparation

Modular equipment architecture tailored for simple syrup dissolving, flavour dosing, and precision continuous in-line carbonation.

Stage 01

Sugar Dissolving Systems

Hot and cold continuous or batch sugar dissolving units producing clarified simple syrup up to 65° Brix.

  • High-shear turbo dissolvers and venturi eductors
  • Plate heat exchanger pasteurizer and cooling stages
  • Dual bag / candle clarification syrup filters
  • Automated inline refractometer Brix measurement
Capacities: 2,000 to 20,000 LPH simple syrup
Stage 02

Final Syrup Preparation Tanks

Sanitary stainless steel mixing and holding tanks equipped for precise concentrate and ingredient blending.

  • AISI 316L internal product contact surfaces (Ra < 0.8 µm)
  • Dimple cooling jackets with polyurethane insulation
  • Bottom magnetic or top mechanical sanitary agitators
  • Load-cell weighing platforms for batch recipe verification
Vessel Sizes: 1,000 L to 15,000 L batch capacity
Stage 03

Deaeration & Carbonation Skids

High-precision continuous water-syrup ratio blending, vacuum deaeration, and micro-bubble CO2 carbonation.

  • Two-stage vacuum spray deaeration (< 0.5 ppm residual O2)
  • Dual Coriolis mass flow ratio dosing (water / syrup)
  • Venturi CO2 gas injector with static mixing chamber
  • Integrated buffer tank with pressure control loop
Carbonation Range: 2.0 to 9.0 g/L (1.0 to 4.5 vol)

04 Technical FAQ

CSD Syrup Room Equipment — Questions & Answers

Answers to common project scoping, modular supply, and automation questions.

What equipment is included in an APS CSD syrup room package?

APS supplies complete equipment packages including simple syrup preparation tanks, final syrup mixing tanks, continuous sugar dissolvers, sanitary centrifugal and positive displacement pumps, mix-proof valve manifolds, vacuum deaeration columns, in-line carbonators, and dedicated CIP sanitization skids.

Can APS supply components for existing syrup room retrofits?

Yes. We source and supply individual modular units — such as high-shear sugar dissolving tanks, flash pasteurizers, Coriolis mass flowmeters, or automated valve matrix skids — designed for seamless integration into existing beverage plant piping.

What automation and control standards are supported?

Systems are configured with Siemens S7-1500 or Allen-Bradley PLCs, industrial SCADA recipe management, inline Brix and CO2 monitoring, and hygienic IP66 stainless steel control cabinets.

What is the standard quotation turnaround for syrup line tenders?

Our engineering desk reviews process P&IDs, capacity specifications (LPH), and equipment datasheets to deliver preliminary technical proposals and budget quotations within 48 hours.
