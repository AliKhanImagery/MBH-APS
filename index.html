05 Procurement & Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct specifications on OEM authenticity, verified manufacturer compatibility, 48h quotation timelines, and international procurement standards.

What machinery lines and process systems does APS engineer and supply?

APS supplies turnkey and modular process packages engineered for Carbonated Soft Drinks (CSD batch syrup rooms and continuous carbonation), Dairy (HTST pasteurization, high-pressure homogenization, centrifugal clarification), Juice & Liquid Food (continuous flash pasteurizers, aseptic blending), Industrial Water Treatment (reverse osmosis skids, media filtration, UV disinfection), Clean-in-Place (CIP automated sanitization skids), and Product Recovery (hygienic pigging systems).

Are all spare parts and components supplied by APS 100% genuine OEM?

Yes. APS sources exclusively through manufacturer-authorized channels and verified primary OEM lines. Every replacement part is cross-referenced against original engineering datasheets and equipment serial numbers to ensure zero tolerance deviation, certified metallurgy (AISI 316L, 304, Hastelloy), FDA-grade elastomers, and complete factory warranty compliance.

Which industrial manufacturers and brands does APS support?

We maintain dedicated procurement channels for global process and automation leaders including Alfa Laval, GEA, SPX Flow, APV, Tetra Pak, Krones, Siemens, Endress+Hauser, Festo, and Danfoss across sanitary pumps, valves, heat exchangers, field instrumentation transmitters, and PLC automation hardware.

What is the standard quotation turnaround time for technical RFQs?

APS targets a 48-hour technical quotation response for all verified enterprise enquiries. Our engineering desk directly reviews your equipment part numbers, process P&IDs, nameplate photos, or project tender documents to deliver accurate pricing, factory lead times, and dispatch logistics.

Which geographic markets does APS supply and service?

Headquartered in Ajman / Dubai, United Arab Emirates, APS actively supplies food and beverage plants, chemical processing facilities, and utility plants across the UAE, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain (GCC), the wider Middle East & North Africa (MENA) region, and international export clients.

What engineering and fabrication services does APS offer?

APS fabricates CIP skids, sanitary stainless steel tanks, electrical control cabinets, and DP panels to client specification. Where a project requires it, APS can also scope automation software and control system integration. All scopes are confirmed directly with the client — contact the engineering desk at sales@apsinox.com to discuss requirements.
