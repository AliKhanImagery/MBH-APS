01 Component Scope

Sanitary Pump Categories & Compatibility

From high-throughput milk and beverage transfer to high-viscosity syrup metering — we supply spares for all major hygienic pump configurations.

Category 01

Sanitary Centrifugal Pumps

High-efficiency transfer pumps for dairy, beer, wine, CIP supply and return, and RO water feed.

  • Single-stage and multi-stage models
  • Self-priming liquid ring variants
  • Semi-open and shrouded impellers
  • Compatible with Alfa Laval LKH / SolidC, Fristam FP, SPX Flow W+
Key Spares: Mechanical seals, backplates, impellers, O-rings
Category 02

Rotary Lobe & Circumferential

Low-shear positive displacement pumps for shear-sensitive fluids, fruit purees, yoghurt, and heavy syrups.

  • Bi-wing, tri-lobe, and multi-lobe rotors
  • Timing gearboxes and heavy-duty shaft assemblies
  • EHEDG & 3-A sanitary design standards
  • Compatible with Alfa Laval SRU / OptiLobe, Waukesha 200 series
Key Spares: Rotor sets, front-loading seals, timing gears, shims
Category 03

Progressive Cavity Pumps

Continuous non-pulsating displacement for highly viscous liquids, tomato paste, chocolate mass, and dosing applications.

  • Precision ground stainless rotors (AISI 316L / hard chrome)
  • Food-grade molded rubber stators (NBR, EPDM, FKM)
  • Pin-joint and open-joint universal assemblies
  • Low pulsation metering consistency
Key Spares: Stators, chrome-plated rotors, connecting rods
02 Seal Technology

Sanitary Mechanical Seals & Elastomers

Reliable seal integrity is the primary defense against product contamination and unplanned plant stoppage.

Face Materials & Seal Configurations

Single Mechanical Seal Standard duty for non-abrasive, non-crystallizing food fluids and clean water.
Flushed / Double Seal For sugar syrups, high-temperature liquids, sterile lines, and CIP chemicals.
Silicon Carbide (SiC/SiC) Extreme hardness, thermal shock resistance, and abrasive particulate endurance.
Carbon / SiC Pairing Self-lubricating low-friction face combination for standard sanitary duties.

All seal sets are supplied with matching wave springs, static drive pins, and precision-seated rotary faces.

Food-Contact Elastomer Compounds

Every O-ring, lip seal, and casing gasket complies strictly with FDA 21 CFR § 177.2600 and 3-A Sanitary Standards:

  • EPDM: Standard for hot water, steam, and mild acid/caustic CIP cycles (-40°C to +140°C).
  • FKM / Viton: Superior resistance to fatty dairy, cooking oils, and aggressive hydrocarbons.
  • PTFE / FFKM: Universal chemical inertness for extreme CIP sanitization and aseptic barrier lines.
Tip: Provide your medium and temperature in the RFQ for recommended seal compounding.

04 Technical FAQ

Sanitary Pump Spares — Questions & Answers

Answers to common procurement, compatibility, and delivery questions regarding sanitary pump replacement components.

How do I identify and request quotes for replacement pump seals?

The fastest way is sending a photograph of the pump manufacturer nameplate (showing model and serial number) and the old seal to sales@apsinox.com or via WhatsApp to +971 553 606698. We cross-reference the manufacturer bill of materials and confirm the exact OEM part numbers.

Which sanitary pump brands and models do you support?

APS sources and supplies replacement spares compatible with Alfa Laval (LKH, SolidC, LKHex, SRU, OptiLobe), Fristam (FP, FPE, FL, FKL), SPX Flow / APV (W+, Puma, DW), and Waukesha Cherry-Burrell equipment. We provide dimensionally verified parts that meet original equipment specifications.

What is the standard quotation response time?

We target a 48-hour quotation response for all verified enterprise enquiries. Our technical engineering desk reviews parts numbers, process specifications, and delivery horizons to deliver competitive pricing and dispatch schedules.

Are sanitary certifications and material test reports provided?

Yes. Upon request, all metallic components (impellers, shafts, casings in AISI 316L) can be supplied with EN 10204 3.1 Material Test Certificates. Elastomers and O-rings are certified compliant with FDA 21 CFR § 177.2600 and 3-A Sanitary Standards.
