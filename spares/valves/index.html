01 Valve Typology

Hygienic Valve Categories & Seal Overhaul Sets

Zero cross-contamination and zero leakage across dairy matrix manifolds, syrup routing, and CIP circuit headers.

Category 01

Double-Seat Mix-Proof Valves

Simultaneous processing of incompatible fluids without risk of cross-contamination in automated beverage and dairy valve matrices.

  • Radial seal rings and axial plug seals
  • Upper and lower stem guide bushings
  • Leakage chamber flush seals and CIP nozzles
  • Compatible with Alfa Laval Unique Mixproof, GEA VARIVENT
Key Spares: Service overhaul kits, radial seals, O-ring packs
Category 02

Single-Seat Shut-Off & Divert

Reliable shut-off, change-over, and diversion in sanitary stainless piping systems for liquid food, CIP, and utility loops.

  • Plug seals with defined compression and metallic stop
  • Stem lip seals and bonnet O-rings
  • Actuator service kits (spring assemblies & air seals)
  • Compatible with Alfa Laval Unique SSV, GEA ECOVENT, SPX SW4
Key Spares: Plug seal rings (EPDM/PTFE), actuator seals
Category 03

Butterfly & Aseptic Diaphragm

Compact isolation for low-viscosity lines and sterile barrier sealing in aseptic filling and sampling ports.

  • Full-bore butterfly valve seat sleeves (EPDM, FKM, Silicone)
  • Precision polished disc stems in AISI 316L
  • Two-piece PTFE/EPDM backed aseptic diaphragms
  • Manual multi-position handles & pneumatic actuators
Key Spares: Seat rings, replacement discs, aseptic diaphragms
02 Control Tops

Valve Control Tops & Pneumatic Spares

Reliable feedback and fast solenoid switching keep automated processing sequences synchronized.

Control Tops & Position Sensing

Sensor Boards & Targets Magnetic hall-sensor targets and electronic position indicator boards.
Solenoid Air Valves 24V DC pilot solenoid valves for rapid pneumatic actuation and de-energizing.
Bus Communication Cards AS-Interface (AS-i), IO-Link, and DeviceNet control module replacements.
Actuator Maintenance Kits Internal piston lip seals, guide rings, and heavy-duty return spring packs.

Spares compatible with Alfa Laval ThinkTop (V70, V50, Classic), GEA T.px, and Burkert digital valve positioners.

Elastomer Compatibility Standards

Process valve seals must endure repeated high-temperature clean-in-place (CIP) and steam-in-place (SIP) cycles without swelling or degradation:

  • EPDM: Hot caustic wash (up to 85°C) and steam sanitization (up to 140°C).
  • FKM (Viton): High fat dairy, edible oils, and high acid beverage lines.
  • PTFE Plug Faces: Extreme abrasion endurance and zero flavour absorption.
Zero Contamination Guarantee: Every seal set is packaged and marked with batch traceability.

04 Technical FAQ

Hygienic Valve Spares — Questions & Answers

Answers to common procurement, compatibility, and delivery questions regarding hygienic valve replacement parts.

How do I identify the correct seal overhaul kit for my process valve?

Provide the valve brand, series name (e.g., Unique SSV, Unique Mixproof, VARIVENT, Delta DA3+), valve nominal size (DN / Inch), and the serial number stamped on the actuator or valve body. You can send photos directly to sales@apsinox.com or via WhatsApp to +971 553 606698.

Which process valve brands and models do you supply spares for?

APS sources and supplies replacement spares compatible with Alfa Laval (Unique SSV, Mixproof, SMP, LKB Butterfly), GEA Tuchenhagen (VARIVENT, ECOVENT), SPX Flow / APV (Delta DA3+, SW4, SD4), and hygienic diaphragm valves from GEMÜ and Saunders.

What is the standard quotation turnaround time?

We target a 48-hour quotation response for all verified enterprise enquiries. Our technical engineering desk reviews parts numbers, process specifications, and delivery horizons to deliver competitive pricing and dispatch schedules.

Do you supply complete pneumatic control tops or individual circuit boards?

Both. We supply complete replacement control heads (compatible with ThinkTop and VARIVENT systems) as well as modular sub-assemblies: 24V DC solenoid valves, visual indication domes, sensor target boards, and AS-i / IO-Link communication modules.
