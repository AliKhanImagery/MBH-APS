01 Thermal Components

Gaskets, Plates & Thermal Replacement Hardware

Maintaining thermal efficiency, preventing inter-channel cross-leakage, and restoring design heat transfer coefficients.

Category 01

PHE Gaskets & Clip Systems

Precision compression-molded elastomer gaskets for glue-free clip-on installations and high-temperature adhesive bonds.

  • Clip-on (Clip-Grip, EasyFix, Loc-In) systems
  • Glue-type gaskets with synthetic resin bonding
  • Diagonal and parallel flow channel port rings
  • Compatible with Alfa Laval ClipLine, GEA Loc-In, APV Paraclip
Key Compounds: Food-grade NBR, high-temp EPDM, FKM (Viton)
Category 02

Heat Transfer Plates

Corrugated chevron-pattern plates engineered for high turbulence, reduced fouling, and maximum thermal recovery.

  • High-theta (acute angle) and low-theta (obtuse angle) corrugations
  • Thickness options from 0.4mm to 0.8mm
  • Die-stamped port alignment collar notches
  • Compatible with Alfa Laval M10/M15, GEA VT/NT, APV H17/R5
Metallurgies: AISI 304, AISI 316L, Titanium Gr. 1, Hastelloy C-276
Category 03

Frame Hardware & Tubular Spares

Mechanical frame compression hardware, port lining sleeves, and tubular bundle replacement parts.

  • High-tensile zinc-plated and stainless tightening bolts
  • Heavy-duty thrust bearing washer assemblies
  • Molded rubber port liners (EPDM, NBR, PTFE)
  • Tubular heat exchanger O-rings and floating head seals
Hardware: Tightening rods, port rings, tube bundle seals
02 Industrial Duty

Application Profiles & Operating Temperatures

Selecting the exact elastomer compound and plate thickness based on fluid characteristics, thermal shock, and CIP chemistry.

Common Thermal Processing Applications

Dairy HTST Pasteurizers Multi-section regeneration, heating, and cooling with FDA-compliant EPDM clip gaskets.
Juice & Beverage Chillers Glycol cooling loops and flash pasteurizers requiring low-theta plates for viscous pulps.
Brewery Wort Coolers Rapid two-stage cooling with treated water and chilled glycol, resistant to hot caustic CIP.
High-Chloride / RO Water Titanium Grade 1 plates to eliminate pitting corrosion from saline or brackish feed water.

Every plate pack is supplied with complete assembly drawings, tightening dimension (A-measure) guidance, and torque specifications.

Elastomer Selection Matrix

Thermal and chemical longevity depends on matching the elastomer grade to your working regime:

  • NBR / Nitrile: -20°C to +135°C. Optimum for fatty dairy, vegetable oils, beer, and general food processing.
  • EPDM: -40°C to +160°C. Resists hot water, steam, aggressive nitric acid, and sodium hydroxide CIP solutions.
  • FKM (Viton): -15°C to +180°C. Extreme chemical resistance to organic solvents and high-strength mineral acids.

04 Technical FAQ

Plate Heat Exchanger Spares — Questions & Answers

Answers to common procurement, elastomer selection, and plate compatibility questions.

How do I identify the exact gasket and plate model for my heat exchanger?

Provide the frame model and serial number from the heat exchanger nameplate (e.g., Alfa Laval FrontLine M10-BFM, GEA VT40, APV R5), the plate count, and the operating media. Photos of the plate chevron pattern or nameplate can be emailed to sales@apsinox.com or sent via WhatsApp to +971 553 606698.

Which plate heat exchanger brands and series do you supply spares for?

APS sources and supplies spares compatible with Alfa Laval (FrontLine, BaseLine, ClipLine, M6, M10, M15, TL10), GEA / Kelvion (VT20, VT40, NT50, NT100, NT150, NT250), SPX Flow / APV (Paraflow R5, H17, SR2, TR9), and Sondex / Danfoss.

What is the standard quotation turnaround time?

Can you supply complete pre-gasketed plate packs?
