Flow Measurement

Hygienic electromagnetic meters and Coriolis mass flow instruments delivering exact volumetric and mass dosing.

  • Electromagnetic flowmeters with PFA liners & platinum electrodes

  • 80 GHz non-contact radar level sensors with hygienic PTFE antennas

04 Technical FAQ

Process Instrumentation — Questions & Answers

Answers to common procurement, calibration, and sensor replacement questions.

Which brands and manufacturers of process instrumentation does APS support?

APS sources and supplies OEM-grade transmitters and sensors compatible with leading industrial automation and process engineering manufacturers including Endress+Hauser, Siemens, IFM Efector, WIKA, Anderson-Negele, Burkert, and Yokogawa.

Are replacement transmitters supplied with calibration certificates?

Yes. Flowmeters, pressure transmitters, and temperature sensors can be supplied with manufacturer factory calibration certificates, 3.1 Material Test Certificates (EN 10204 3.1) for wetted stainless parts, and hygienic conformity documentation (3-A, EHEDG, FDA).

What process connections and hygienic fittings are available?

Sensors are supplied with standard hygienic connections including Tri-Clamp (ISO 2852 / DIN 32676), Varivent, DIN 11851 milk pipe couplings, SMS 1145, and aseptic weld-in sockets with internal Ra < 0.4 µm finish.

What is the typical quotation timeline for instrument replacement parts?

Submit the instrument tag, complete order code, or a photo of the transmitter nameplate to sales@apsinox.com to receive verified specification cross-referencing and formal commercial pricing within 48 hours.
